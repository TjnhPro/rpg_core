## Limitations and required next decision

possible dynamic callback path or detect arbitrary equivalent hidden arithmetic,
dead calls, indirect state writes, proxies or stores outside the finite manifest.

no checker repair was needed. Runtime evidence covers the recorded caller
adapters/scenarios and retained suites, not arbitrary caller mutation of exposed
Legacy containers. No deterministic guarantee is claimed for caller callbacks
beyond these traces. The complete golden is deliberately larger than summary
fixtures because it retains nested observations at notification boundaries.
